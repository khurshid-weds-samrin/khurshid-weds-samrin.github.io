the nikah, explained

  1. Khutbah-e-NikahA short sermon of Qur’anic verses on marriage, taqwa and mutual rights.
  2. MahrThe groom’s gift to the bride, agreed upon and hers alone: a sign of honour.
  3. Ijab & QuboolOffer and acceptance. Each gives consent: “Qubool hai.”
  4. WitnessesTwo witnesses attest, and the nikahnama is signed.
  5. DuaAll present raise their hands in prayer for the couple’s blessed life together.
